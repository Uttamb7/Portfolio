About me / why I build

The first computer
was on the other side
of a window.

I grew up in a refugee camp in Nepal after my parents fled Bhutan. School was never something my family could take for granted. Work and survival came first, and my father spent most of his time working to keep our family moving forward.

As a child, I saw a computer for the first time through the window of a house I was not allowed to enter. I still remember the sound of the keyboard. I walked home thinking that one day I would not only use a computer, but build with one.

Resettling in the United States meant starting again and learning English from scratch. Curiosity became my way forward: asking questions, taking things apart, and learning how systems work.

Technology showed me what could be possible. I want to build systems that make possibility real for someone else.

Today I focus on backend, frontend, full-stack, and cloud engineering, and I am looking for a team where thoughtful software can make a measurable impact.
